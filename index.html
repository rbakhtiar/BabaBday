A letter for Baba 15 · 10 · 2026

Dear Baba,

You work so hard for us, encourage us when we have dreams, and always find ways to make us feel happy. I may not always say it out loud, but I notice these things, and they mean a lot to me.

You are my role model. I admire how smart, caring, and hardworking you are. You show me that doing your best, looking after the people you love, and continuing to learn are things worth being proud of.

Some of my favourite memories are the trips to Dubai and Lahore, along with celebrating your birthdays together. These moments are special to me, and I hope we make many more memories like them.

I love how you are interested in cars, technology, AI, and travelling. You inspire me to explore ideas of my own, keep creating, and believe that I can turn what I imagine into something real.

I made this little website myself because I wanted to give you something personal. It isn't just a birthday page. It is a small way of saying thank you for everything you do for us.

Happy Birthday, Baba. I hope this next year brings you happiness, good health, wonderful journeys, and many reasons to smile.

With love and appreciation, YOUR SON
