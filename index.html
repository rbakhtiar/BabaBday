For Baba, with love 15 · 10 · 2026

Dear Baba,

You work so hard for us, encourage us to follow our dreams, and make us feel happy. I might not always say it, but I notice what you do, and I appreciate it more than you know.

You are my role model. I admire how smart and caring you are, and how hard you work. You teach me that there is value in trying your best, learning new things, and looking after the people who matter.

I will always remember our trips to Dubai and Lahore, and celebrating your other birthdays together. Those experiences are part of our story, and I hope we make many more memories in the years ahead.

I love how you enjoy cars, AI, technology, and travelling. You inspire me to explore ideas, build things of my own, and imagine what might be possible in the future.

I made this website because I wanted to give you something that came from me. It is a little reminder that the work you do, the encouragement you give, and the happiness you bring to our lives do not go unnoticed.

Happy Birthday, Baba. I wish you happiness, good health, exciting journeys, and many wonderful years ahead.

With love and appreciation,YOUR SON
